Why this website exists

This project was built to prove that HTML alone can still create a memorable, structured, and well-crafted experience when content is arranged with care.

Core principles

Clarity first

Visitors should instantly understand the purpose of each section and feel guided through the page.

Accessibility matters

Meaningful headings, labeled forms, and clear navigation make the experience stronger for everyone.

Storytelling over decoration

Even without CSS, a thoughtful narrative can make a site feel immersive and polished.

Milestones

  1. Built a strong hero section with a clear message.
  2. Added rich content blocks for personality and depth.
  3. Structured forms and navigation for a professional finish.

Quick note

The strongest websites are not always the flashiest; they are the ones that communicate clearly and leave a lasting impression.
